Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026. Plain-language draft: have it reviewed for your jurisdiction and add a contact address.

What we store

Your transactions, accounts, goals, debts, holdings and settings. Without an account, this data stays on your device only.

Accounts and sync

If you sign up, your username (saved as an internal email-style ID), profile name, password and budget data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) so they can sync between your devices. Passwords are handled by Firebase and never visible to us.

What we do not do

We show no ads, run no analytics or trackers, and never sell or share your data. Prices for holdings are typed in by you; the app does not look them up online.

Your controls

Export a backup or CSV, reset all data, or log out from Settings. To delete your account and cloud data, contact the app owner.

Notifications and PIN

Reminders are generated on your device. Your PIN is stored only as a hash on your device.
